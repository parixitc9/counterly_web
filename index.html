Real profit

Bought at ₹900, then at ₹1,000. Sold at the rate you agreed. What did you make?

Counterly keeps every purchase as its own lot and sells from the oldest first. Profit comes from what you paid for the exact units that left the shop. A missing cost is flagged, never counted as zero.

What it does

Made for the counter, not the accountant.

Bill in seconds, at the price you agreed

Search by name or SKU (F2), or scan with any USB barcode scanner. Change the rate on one bill without touching the master price. Cash, UPI or card, with change worked out. The cart can't sell stock you don't have.

Sell by the box or by the kilo

Drums, bags and loose stock on the same bill. Quantities up to three decimals for kg, litre and metre, with exact costing.

Know what every unit cost

Each purchase is its own lot: quantity, cost, date, supplier and batch. See the weighted average cost of what's left on the shelf.

Fix a wrong bill, keep the record

Cancel with a reason. The bill number stays, stock goes back to the lots it came from, and reports leave it out.

Every customer, every bill

Business or personal, owner name, GSTIN, billing and shipping address, total purchases and full history. Find anyone by name or mobile.

Invoice to WhatsApp and email

Sent after checkout through your own WhatsApp Business API and email account. A queue with retries, no double sends and consent tracking.

Your shop at a glance

Today's sales, bills, units and low stock. Sales over time, payment mix, top products, stock health and profit, for any dates.

A backup every day, automatically

A full copy of your shop data every day, with the last 14 days kept. Restore it on the same PC or a new one, or export everything to CSV.

Your data stays on your PC

A local database, and billing works without internet. Your shop name and logo sit on the app, the invoices and the desktop shortcut.

GST tax invoices

CGST, SGST and IGST, worked out for you.

HSN/SAC and GST rate on every product. In-state bills split into CGST and SGST, and a buyer GSTIN from another state switches the bill to IGST. Each A4 PDF carries your logo, GSTIN, place of supply and page numbers.

Questions

Before you install.

More answers in the documentation →
Does it need internet?

No. Billing, stock and reports work offline, and the license key activates offline. Internet is only used to send invoices on WhatsApp or email.

Where is my data, and is it safe?

In a database on your own PC. Counterly saves a full backup every day to Documents\Counterly Backups and keeps the last 14. If OneDrive backs up your Documents folder, the backups go with it.

You can restore a backup on the same PC or a new one, and export every record to CSV. Email and WhatsApp credentials are encrypted with Windows secure storage.

Windows says "Unknown publisher". Is that normal?

Yes, for now. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen asks first. Click More info, then Run anyway. To check the file is ours, compare its SHA-256 with the one on the download page.

What do I need for WhatsApp invoices?

Your own WhatsApp Business Cloud API account from Meta, with an approved message template. Email uses your own mail account (SMTP). Counterly shows when WhatsApp or your mail server has accepted each message.

Is it e-invoice ready?

It makes GST tax invoices with HSN, CGST, SGST and IGST, and place of supply. It does not generate government e-invoices (IRN) or e-way bills. Your CA should confirm the invoice format for your business.

What can't it do yet?

It runs on one Windows PC, with no Mac, mobile or multi-branch version. It shows gross profit, not accounts or net profit. Credit balances (udhaar), partial returns and credit notes aren't included yet.
