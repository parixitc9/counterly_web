Licensing questions

The fine print, in plain words.

Read the license terms →
What happens when my license ends?

Billing and other business actions lock. Your products, stock, customers and bills stay exactly where they are, and "Export my records" still works. Enter a new key and you carry on.

Can I use one license on two PCs?

No. A key is bound to the Hardware ID of one PC. Each PC that bills needs its own license.

I'm moving to a new PC. What do I do?

Copy your latest backup to the new PC and restore it, then send us the new PC's Hardware ID for a key. The backup guide has the steps. Email and WhatsApp credentials need to be entered again.

Do new versions cost extra?

New versions install over the old one and keep your existing license and records.

Is WhatsApp sending included?

The feature is included. Sending needs your own WhatsApp Business Platform account from Meta, and any charges Meta makes for messages are between you and Meta.

Can I see it before I buy?

The features page shows every module, and the home page plays an example bill from start to finish. For anything else, talk to us.
