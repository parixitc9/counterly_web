Solutions

Built for businesses that buy in bulk and sell by the rate.

Counterly started inside a chemical and materials wholesaler. Drums, bags, loose kilos, rates agreed on the phone and business buyers with GSTINs are what it was made for.

  • Wholesalers and distributors
  • Retail shops
  • Small factories
  • Is it right for you?

Wholesalers and distributors

Every rate negotiated. Every margin known.

You buy the same item at a different price every month and sell it at whatever the customer agreed to. Counterly keeps both sides straight.

  1. 01

    Receive

    Record each purchase at its own cost, with supplier and batch.

  2. 02

    Bill

    Set the agreed rate on the bill. The master price stays put.

  3. 03

    Know

    Profit per bill, from the lots that were actually sold.

  • Business customers. GSTIN, owner name, billing and shipping addresses, saved once and reused.
  • Out-of-state buyers. A GSTIN from another state switches the bill to IGST automatically.
  • Per-bill rates. Negotiate on every bill without touching your price list.
  • Bill-by-bill profit. Sales before GST, cost of goods sold and margin for each invoice.
  • Invoices sent for you. The A4 PDF goes to the buyer on WhatsApp or email after checkout.

Retail shops

A fast counter that never sells what isn't there.

Scan, take payment, hand over the bill. The stock count is right at the end of the day without anyone counting it.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Any USB barcode scanner adds the product with that SKU.

  2. 02

    Pay

    Cash with change worked out, UPI or card.

  3. 03

    Restock

    The dashboard lists products at or below their minimum.

  • Walk-in sales. Bill without a customer, or pick a saved one by name or mobile.
  • Stock-aware cart. Out-of-stock items can't be added, and quantities can't pass what's on the shelf.
  • Daily view. Today's sales, bills, units sold and recent transactions on one screen.
  • Payment mix. See how much came in as cash, UPI and card, for any dates.
  • Works offline. The counter keeps billing when the internet doesn't.

Small factories

Sell by the kilo, cost to the gram.

Loose material, part-used drums and batches from different suppliers, with exact costing all the way through.

  1. 01

    Stock in

    Receive raw or finished goods by batch and supplier.

  2. 02

    Sell loose

    2.5 kg or 0.75 L on the same bill as full bags.

  3. 03

    Cost it

    Oldest lot first, to three decimal places.

  • Three decimals. Quantities like 12.5 kg or 0.75 L, for kg, litre and metre.
  • Batches on every lot. Track supplier, batch and receipt date for each purchase.
  • Stock reductions. Use Adjust stock to take out material that's used or written off.
  • Average cost. The weighted average cost of what's left, worked out from the remaining lots.
  • HSN on every line. The right code and GST rate on each invoice line.

Is it right for you?

Who it's for, and who it isn't for yet.

A good fit

  • You run one shop, warehouse or factory counter on a Windows PC.
  • You sell to businesses with GSTINs as well as walk-in customers.
  • You buy the same items at changing prices and want true profit.
  • You negotiate rates bill by bill.
  • You want your records on your own PC, without a cloud account.

Not a fit yet

  • You run several branches that share one set of stock.
  • You need full accounting, expenses or net profit.
  • You sell on credit and track customer balances (udhaar).
  • You need partial returns, credit notes or government e-invoices.
  • You want to bill from a phone or a Mac.
