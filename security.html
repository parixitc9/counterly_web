Security and data

Your shop's data stays on your shop's PC.

Counterly has no cloud account and no servers holding your records. This page explains exactly where your data lives, what leaves the PC and when, and how the app protects both.

Where data lives

Three folders on your own PC.

The shop database

Products, lots, customers, bills and settings, in one local database file under %APPDATA%\counterly.

Invoice PDFs

Saved to your Documents folder and named by invoice number. Unsafe file names and locations are rejected.

Daily backups

A full copy every day in Documents\Counterly Backups, last 14 kept. If OneDrive backs up Documents, the copies go with it.

What leaves your PC

Only what you choose to send, to services you set up.

Counterly sends no usage analytics and doesn't update itself. The license is checked offline. The only connections it makes are the ones in this table.

Credentials

Passwords and tokens are locked away.

The email password and WhatsApp access token you enter are needed to send invoices, so they're treated as the most sensitive data in the app.

  • Encrypted at rest. With Windows secure storage, tied to your Windows account.
  • Never shown again. Once saved, a credential isn't sent back to the screen. Leaving the field blank keeps it.
  • Errors without secrets. Provider errors are shown without the access token.
  • Not in the installer. No sender passwords, tokens or private licensing material ship with Counterly.
  • On a new PC. Credentials have to be entered again, because the encryption belongs to the old Windows account.

Licensing

An offline key that can't lock you out of your data.

Activation needs no internet and no account. Expiry pauses billing, never your records.

  • Signed keys. Each key is signed, and the app checks the signature with a public key. The private signing key never leaves our side.
  • One PC. A key is bound to the Hardware ID of one computer, a hashed identifier generated on that PC.
  • Expiry keeps data. Business actions lock, records stay, and renewing restores everything.
  • Export anytime. "Export my records" works on the activation screen, licensed or not.
  • Clock protection. Setting the clock back doesn't extend a license. A new key resets the clock record if it was wrong.

Record integrity

Records that don't quietly change.

A bill is a business record. Counterly is built so one never changes after the fact.

  • All or nothing. A checkout saves the bill, lines, payment, lot allocations and stock changes together, or not at all.
  • Snapshots. Each bill keeps a copy of the shop and customer details at the time of sale.
  • Exact money. Amounts are kept in paise, and values that can't be represented exactly are rejected.
  • No silent deletes. Sold products can't be deleted, and wrong bills are cancelled with a reason.
  • Honest reports. A loading error never shows as zero sales, and missing costs are never counted as zero.

App hardening

Locked down where desktop apps are usually open.

Counterly is built on Electron. These protections are switched on in the packaged app.

  • No debugging back door. The Node inspector, the alternate Node entry point and NODE_OPTIONS are disabled.
  • Tamper checks. The app loads only from its packaged archive, and the archive's integrity is checked.
  • Contained windows. Pop-ups and navigation away from the app are blocked, and every internal request checks it came from a trusted window.
  • Safe invoices. Customer and product text is escaped, and the invoice renderer can't load outside or local files.
  • Safe exports. CSV cells that look like spreadsheet formulas are neutralised.
