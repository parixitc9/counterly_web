Getting started

A new installation starts empty: no products, customers, bills or shop details. Work through these steps once, in order.

  1. Download and install Counterly, then open it.
  2. Copy the Hardware ID from the activation screen and activate your license.
  3. In Settings → App, save your shop name, logo, address, phone, GSTIN and invoice note.
  4. Add your products with their selling price, cost and minimum stock.
  5. Record received stock in Inventory, one lot per purchase cost.
  6. Add business or personal customers as you need them.
  7. If you want invoices sent automatically, set up email and WhatsApp, and send one test invoice on each.
  8. Use POS for billing, Inventory for restocking, Sales for saved invoices and Reports for analysis.

Activate your license

The activation screen shows your PC's Hardware ID with a copy button. Send it to us with the term you want (1, 3, 6 or 12 months). We send back a signed key: paste it into the activation field. Activation works offline.

  • A key works only on the PC whose Hardware ID it was issued for.
  • When a license expires, business actions lock but every record stays. A new key for the same PC unlocks it again.
  • Export my records on the activation screen exports your data at any time, with or without a license.

If the PC's clock once ran ahead and was then corrected, Counterly reports a clock rollback. A new, unused activation key restores access and resets the clock record.

Set up your shop

Open Settings → App and save your shop or business name, address, phone, GSTIN, invoice note and logo. Billing stays locked until the name and address are saved, so no invoice goes out without a real seller on it.

  • The logo must be a small PNG. It appears on the sidebar, the window title and icon, and every new invoice.
  • Saving the name and logo creates a desktop launcher named after your shop. Click Show shop app file to find it. It opens the same records.
  • Add a GSTIN to get "Tax Invoice" titles, place of supply and CGST/SGST/IGST lines.

Add products

Each product needs a name, a unique SKU, a unit and a selling price. Optional fields: category, HSN/SAC code (4 to 8 digits), GST rate (18% unless you change it) and minimum stock level.

  • For barcode scanning, enter the number printed under the barcode as the SKU.
  • Enter a profit percentage to have the selling price worked out from cost.
  • Initial stock and its cost can be entered when you create the product.
  • Products that have been sold can't be deleted, because old invoices refer to them.

Receive stock

In Inventory, use Adjust stock to add or reduce stock. Each addition records a quantity, purchase cost, date, and optionally a supplier and batch. A purchase at a different cost is saved as a new lot.

Breakdown shows every lot for a product: what came in, when, at what cost, and how much is left. The average cost shown is worked out from what's left:

average cost = total remaining lot cost ÷ total remaining quantity

Sales always take stock from the oldest lot first and save the exact cost used, so later purchases never change past profit.

Make a bill

  1. In POS / Billing, pick a saved customer, add a new one, or leave it as a walk-in sale.
  2. Search by name or SKU (press F2 to jump to search), or scan a barcode. Click a product to add it.
  3. Set quantities (up to three decimals), and change the rate or GST % on any line if needed. A changed rate applies to this bill only.
  4. Choose Cash, UPI or Card. For cash, enter the amount received to see the change.
  5. Click Checkout. The bill is saved, stock is updated and the A4 PDF can be saved or sent.

The bill date is today unless you pick an earlier one. Future dates aren't allowed. If saving the PDF fails, make it again from Sales without charging twice.

How GST is worked out

Rates are entered before GST. The taxable amount is the sum of quantity × rate for every line, kept exactly to the paisa. Then, for each GST rate on the bill:

SituationTax lines on the bill
Shop has a GSTIN, buyer has none or is in the same stateCGST and SGST, each at half the rate
Shop has a GSTIN, business buyer's GSTIN is from another stateIGST at the full rate
Shop has no GSTINOne tax line per rate

The state is read from the first two digits of each GSTIN, for example 24 for Gujarat and 27 for Maharashtra.

Counterly does not generate government e-invoices (IRN and signed QR) or e-way bills. Confirm the invoice format with your tax advisor.

Cancel a bill

For a bill entered by mistake, open Sales and click ✕ on the bill, then give a reason.

  • The bill keeps its number and stays in Sales marked "Cancelled".
  • Its items go back to the exact lots they came from.
  • It no longer counts in sales, profit, the dashboard or customer totals.
  • A cancelled bill can't be sent, and cancelling can't be undone. Save its PDF again to get the cancelled copy.

Email invoices

Counterly sends email through your own mail account using SMTP with an app password. It doesn't support signing in with Google or Microsoft.

  1. Get SMTP details and an app password from your email provider.
  2. In Settings → WhatsApp & Email, enter the SMTP server, port, username, sender address and app password. Port 587 uses STARTTLS; port 465 uses TLS.
  3. Turn on automatic email, save, and click the connection check.

For Gmail accounts that allow app passwords, use smtp.gmail.com, port 587, and your full address as both username and sender. Use a Google app password, not your normal password.

The connection check proves the login works, not that mail reaches inboxes. Customers without an email address are simply skipped.

WhatsApp invoices

WhatsApp sending uses the WhatsApp Business Platform Cloud API from Meta. A phone number on its own isn't enough. You set up the account with Meta, and Counterly uses it.

  1. Set up a WhatsApp Business Platform sender with Meta. Note the phone number ID and a business access token that can send messages.
  2. In WhatsApp Manager, create an invoice template with a document header, a fixed body and no body variables, for example "Thank you for your purchase. Your invoice is attached." Wait for Meta to approve it.
  3. In Settings → WhatsApp & Email, enter the phone number ID, template name, template language, Graph API version and access token. Turn on automatic WhatsApp, save and check the connection.
  4. In Customers, record each customer's mobile number and their agreement to receive bills on WhatsApp. Consent is off until you turn it on.

Indian 10-digit mobile numbers get the 91 prefix automatically. Other numbers need their country code. Changing a customer's mobile or turning consent off stops any queued WhatsApp sending to the old number.

The connection check confirms your sender, not template approval or delivery. Send one real test invoice before using it with customers.

Sending status

After checkout, each enabled channel is queued. Counterly checks the queue about every 45 seconds while it's open and online, and picks up again when you reopen it. Sales → Share shows each invoice's status:

StatusMeaning
QueuedWaiting to be sent.
SendingBeing handed to WhatsApp or your mail server now.
Provider acceptedWhatsApp or your mail server took the message. It won't be sent again from here.
FailedSending didn't work. Temporary connection problems are retried a limited number of times, and you can retry by hand.
UnknownIt may or may not have gone. Check with the customer, then confirm a possible duplicate before retrying.

"Provider accepted" doesn't mean the customer has read it: Counterly doesn't receive read receipts. Turning automatic sending off pauses the queue for that channel; sending by hand from Sales still works.

Backup and restore

Once a day while Counterly is open, a full copy of your shop database is saved as Documents\Counterly Backups\counterly-YYYY-MM-DD.db. The latest 14 days are kept. Settings → Backup & data shows the last backup and warns if one failed or is more than two days old.

Restore on the same PC

  1. Close Counterly.
  2. Copy the backup you want to %APPDATA%\counterly\pos_data.db, replacing the file that's there.
  3. Open Counterly.

Move to a new PC

  1. Install Counterly on the new PC and restore the backup there, as above.
  2. Send us the new PC's Hardware ID and activate the key we send.
  3. Enter your email and WhatsApp credentials again. They're encrypted for the old PC and can't be moved.

Export to CSV in the same tab saves products, stock receipts, customers, bills and bill items as spreadsheet-ready files for your accountant.

Clear the workspace

To start completely fresh, open Settings → Clear this workspace, type ERASE ALL and choose Erase all data and activation.

This removes products, stock and lot history, customers, bills, shop details, logo, settings and the saved activation. A recovery backup is made first, and its location is shown afterwards. You'll need to enter your license key again.

Troubleshooting

"Windows protected your PC"

The installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Check the file's SHA-256 first using the steps on the download page.

Checkout is disabled

Save your shop name and address in Settings → App. Billing waits for them.

A database message appears

The message says what's wrong: another program is using the file, the file is read-only, the disk is full, or the file is damaged. For a damaged file, restore from a backup.

WhatsApp stopped sending

If the access token has expired, Counterly says so. Create a new token with Meta and enter it in Settings → WhatsApp & Email. Rate limits are retried automatically.

Billing feels slow

Catalogues above roughly 3,000 to 5,000 products feel slow in the billing grid and product lists. Search instead of scrolling.

Contact support with your Counterly version, what you were doing, and a screenshot of any message.
