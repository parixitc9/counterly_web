About

Software for the shop counter, built from one.

Counterly makes billing and stock software for Indian shops, wholesalers and small factories. One product, done carefully.

Our story

It started with a wholesaler's question.

Counterly began as a tool for a chemical and materials wholesaler. Drums and bags, loose kilos, business buyers with GSTINs, rates agreed on the phone, and purchase prices that changed every month.

The question it had to answer every day was simple: on this bill, at this rate, what did we actually make? Answering it honestly takes more than today's purchase price.

So Counterly keeps every purchase as its own lot, sells the oldest stock first, and saves the exact cost on every bill. It runs on the shop's own PC and keeps working when the internet doesn't. Everything else, from GST invoices to WhatsApp sending, grew from that same counter.

What we believe

Four rules we build by.

Your data, your PC

Records live on the shop's computer. We never receive them, and an expired license never deletes them.

Numbers you can trust

A missing cost is flagged, never counted as zero. A loading error never shows as zero sales.

Say what it can't do

"Provider accepted" means accepted, not delivered. No e-invoicing means we say so, on the page you buy from.

Fast, and offline

Open in about a second, check out in milliseconds, and keep billing with no internet at all.

How we ship

Every release is checked before it reaches a counter.
