POS / Billing

Bill in seconds, at the price you agreed.

One screen for the person at the counter: find the product, set the rate, take payment and save the invoice.

  • Search or scan. Find products by name or SKU, with F2 to jump to search. A USB barcode scanner works without setup: the product whose SKU matches is added.
  • Per-bill rates. Change the selling price on one bill. The master price, average cost and lot history stay as they are.
  • GST per line. Each line starts with the product's GST rate, and you can change it on the bill.
  • Loose quantities. Up to three decimals, for kg, litre and metre.
  • No overselling. Every add and quantity change checks stock. Out-of-stock products can't be added.
  • Cash, UPI or card. Cash checkout asks for the amount received and works out the change.
  • Backdated bills. The date defaults to today and can be set earlier. Future dates are rejected.
  • Walk-in or saved customer. Search by name, owner or mobile, or add a new customer without leaving the bill.

Products

A catalogue that protects your history.

The product master feeds billing and stock. Changing it never rewrites a bill you've already issued.

  • The basics. Name, SKU, category and unit, such as Box, kg, L or m.
  • Unique SKUs. Two products can't share a SKU, even with different capitals.
  • Tax details. HSN/SAC code of 4 to 8 digits, and a GST rate that starts at 18%.
  • Pricing help. Enter a profit percentage and the selling price is worked out from cost.
  • Minimum stock. Set a level, and the product shows up as low stock on the dashboard.
  • Search and sort. By name or SKU, and sort by name, stock or selling price.
  • Safe edits. A product that has been sold can't be deleted, so old invoices stay accurate.

Know what every unit actually cost.

Each purchase is kept as its own lot. Sales take from the oldest lot first and save the exact cost they used, so profit stays true even when prices move.

  • Every receipt is a lot. Quantity, purchase cost, date, supplier and batch.
  • Breakdown view. See what was added, when, at what cost, and how much of each lot is left.

  • Safe checkouts. If a multi-item checkout fails partway, every change is rolled back.

Customers

Every customer, every bill.

Business and walk-in buyers in one place, with the details each invoice needs.

  • Business or personal. Name, owner name, mobile, optional email, GSTIN for businesses.
  • Two addresses. Billing address, and an optional shipping address.
  • Full history. Total purchases, last purchase date and every bill, each one viewable.
  • Quick search. By customer name, owner name or mobile, with a Business or Personal filter.
  • WhatsApp consent. Recorded per customer and off until they agree.
  • Bills keep their snapshot. Editing a customer later doesn't change bills already issued.

Sales and corrections

Fix a wrong bill without losing the record.

The sales register keeps every bill. A mistake is cancelled with a reason, never quietly deleted.

  • Bill register. Bill number, customer, date, payment method and amount, with a From and To date filter.
  • Per bill. View it, save the PDF, or share it on WhatsApp or email.
  • Cancel with a reason. The number stays and the bill is marked "Cancelled".
  • Stock goes back. Items return to the exact lots they came from.
  • Reports stay true. Cancelled bills leave sales, profit, the dashboard and customer totals.

WhatsApp and email

The invoice reaches the customer after checkout.

Through your own WhatsApp Business account and your own email account. Nothing is sent through us.

"Provider accepted" means WhatsApp or your mail server took the message. An Unknown result waits for you to confirm before it's sent again.

  • Email. Your own SMTP account with an app password, on port 587 or 465.
  • WhatsApp. The WhatsApp Business Platform Cloud API, with your approved invoice template.
  • Automatic or by hand. Sent after checkout, or from Sales whenever you choose.
  • Consent first. WhatsApp goes only to customers who agreed, on the mobile saved on the bill.
  • No double sends. A queue with limited retries that picks up again when you reopen the app.
  • Protected credentials. Encrypted with Windows secure storage and never shown again.

Settings and backup

It's your shop's app, and your shop's data.

Your name and logo on everything, a backup every day, and your records always exportable.

Backup and restore guide →
  • Shop identity. Name, address, phone, GSTIN, invoice note and logo.
  • Your brand. On the sidebar, window title, icon, invoices and a desktop launcher named after your shop.
  • Daily backup. A full copy in Documents\Counterly Backups, with the last 14 days kept.
  • Backup status. Shows the last backup and warns if one failed or is more than two days old.
  • CSV export. Products, stock receipts, customers, bills and bill items, ready for your accountant.
  • Always exportable. "Export my records" works on the activation screen, even without a license.
  • Clean start. Clear this workspace by typing ERASE ALL. A recovery backup is made first.

Honest limits

What Counterly doesn't do yet.

Better you hear it from us before you buy.

  • Government e-invoicing. No IRN, signed QR or e-way bills.
  • Returns and credit notes. A mistaken bill is cancelled as a whole.
  • Credit sales. Customer balances (udhaar) aren't tracked.
  • Accounts. Reports show gross profit. Expenses and net profit aren't included.
  • Camera scanning. USB barcode scanners work. Webcams don't.
  • Read receipts. Counterly knows when WhatsApp or email accepted a message, not when it was read.
  • Other platforms. One Windows PC. No Mac, mobile or multi-branch version.
  • Very large catalogues. Above roughly 3,000 to 5,000 products, the billing grid gets slow.
