Privacy policy

The short version: your shop records stay on your PC, and we never receive them.

Last updated 20 October 2026

On this page

  2. The Counterly app
  3. Services you connect

Summary

  • The Counterly app stores your shop's records in a database on your own computer. We don't receive, see or store them.
  • The app sends no usage analytics and doesn't check for updates.
  • Invoices leave your PC only when you send them through an email or WhatsApp account you set up.
  • When you buy a license, we receive your Hardware ID and the contact details you message us with.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

The Counterly app

Counterly keeps the following on your computer only: your shop details and logo, products, stock lots, customers (including names, mobile numbers, email addresses, GSTINs and addresses you enter), bills, invoice PDFs, reports, settings and daily backups.

The database is stored under %APPDATA%\counterly, invoices in your Documents folder, and backups in Documents\Counterly Backups. If Windows syncs your Documents folder to OneDrive or another service, those files are copied under that service's terms, not ours.

Email passwords and WhatsApp access tokens are encrypted with Windows secure storage and are never shown back on screen.

You are responsible for the customer information you record in Counterly, including having your customers' agreement before sending them invoices on WhatsApp. The app records that agreement per customer.

Services you connect

If you turn on invoice sending, Counterly connects directly from your PC to:

  • Your email provider, through the SMTP account you enter, to send the invoice PDF to the customer's email address.
  • Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, using the account and access token you enter, to send the invoice PDF to the customer's mobile number.

These are your accounts with those providers. What they do with messages is covered by their own terms and privacy policies. Nothing is sent through our servers.

Buying a license

To issue a license key, we need the Hardware ID shown on the activation screen. It's a hashed identifier generated on your computer, and it doesn't contain your name or your shop data.

When you contact us on WhatsApp or email, we receive whatever you include, such as your name, shop name, mobile number and email address. We use it only to issue and renew licenses and to answer your questions. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

This website

  • The site sets no cookies and includes no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page opens.
  • The company hosting this site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested.
  • The installer downloads from GitHub, which handles that request under its own privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp links open WhatsApp, and email links open your mail app. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Your choices

  • Take your data out. Export everything to CSV from Settings → Backup & data, or with "Export my records" on the activation screen.
  • Delete it. Settings → Clear this workspace removes all records from the app. You can also delete the backup and invoice folders.
  • Contact details we hold. Ask us to see, correct or delete the contact details you've sent us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For any privacy question, contact us.
